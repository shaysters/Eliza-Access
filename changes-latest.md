# Eliza Access 1.0.5

Run **ElizaAccessInstaller.exe** (or the one you already have) to install or update. Close the game first. The full guide, with every key, is in the README on the GitHub page, and also comes in the download as ElizaAccess-README.html and ElizaAccess-README.md.

## Changes since 1.0.4

- Settings: sliders read their value again as you arrow through them, for example "Effect Volume, slider, 90%". Since the game's last update they only read their name.
- Menus respond faster. Pressing one arrow key and then quickly the other no longer has a short delay before the second press registers, and speech starts a little sooner after every key press.
- Hariman is now described as a man. His description had him as a woman.
- Other description corrections, after checking every character, avatar and location against the artwork:
  - Left and right hands and the way people are turned were mixed up for Rainer, Soren, Rae, Erlend and Evelyn in her suit.
  - Erlend is described as a young man in his twenties.
  - Darren's pose is right: hunched, with his fists together in front of him.
  - The picture of Nora at her equipment now names Evelyn as the person with her.
  - The restaurant, the night skyline and the nightclub name the people in them: Evelyn and Soren, Evelyn, and Nora.
  - The poster in Rae's house is for Ionesco's play Rhinoceros.
  - Rainer's avatar is the Skandha logo, and Soren's avatar shows an older man with a moustache.
  - The light rail car is no longer called empty, and the Eliza lobby has six lamps, not five.
- All of these corrections are in every language.
